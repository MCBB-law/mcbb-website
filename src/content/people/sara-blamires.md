Sara E. Blamires is an associate at Manning Curtis Bradshaw & Bednar, where her practice focuses on premises liability, transportation, and construction defect matters. While in law school, Ms. Blamires was a Summer Intern with the Colorado 4th Judicial District Attorney’s Office, a Law Clerk with the Utah Attorney General’s Office Criminal Appeals Division, a Summer Associate with Hall & Evans, and Law Clerk with a small firm in Provo, Utah. She was also an Extern at a firm in Auckland, New Zealand, selected as a participant for the BYU Law International Commercial Arbitration Academy in Genva, Switzerland, and a selected for a Global Law Seminar in Berlin, Germany.

Outside of work, Ms. Blamires enjoys snowboarding, hiking, rock climbing, and practicing yoga.
