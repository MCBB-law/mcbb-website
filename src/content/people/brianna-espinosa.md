Brianna N. Espinosa (formerly Brianna N. Landon) is an associate at Manning Curtis Bradshaw & Bednar, where her practice focuses on tax controversy, commercial litigation, employment law, and municipal defense. Her tax practice includes representing businesses and governmental entities in disputes and challenges involving the Utah State Tax Commission and other taxing authorities, as well as in tax litigation and administrative proceedings concerning the interpretation, application, and enforcement of state and local tax laws. Before attending law school, Brianna was a tax consulting intern at Ernst & Young. During law school, she externed for the Honorable Jennifer A. Mabey of Utah’s Fourth District Court and served as president of the BYU Tax Policy Association, where she led initiatives exploring the intersection of tax policy and public interest.

A native of Ammon, Idaho, Brianna is a BYU sports fan and enjoys playing basketball and pickleball in her free time.
